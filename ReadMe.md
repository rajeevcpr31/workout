# 🍽️ Basic Nutrition Explained

Think of your body like a **car**, and food like **fuel + spare parts**.

---

## ⚡ What Are Calories?

Calories are simply **units of energy** in food — "how much fuel is in this tank."

- Everything you eat has a calorie count
- Move more or build muscle → you burn more calories
- Eat more calories than you burn → you gain weight
- Eat less than you burn → you lose weight

Your body uses this energy to:
- Keep your heart and organs working
- Walk and move
- Digest food
- Exercise
- Build and repair muscle

**Examples of calorie-containing foods:** Rice, Roti, Milk, Eggs, Oil — all of these contain calories.

---

## ⚖️ Maintenance, Surplus & Deficit

| Term | What It Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Maintenance Calories** | The amount of food energy needed to keep your body weight stable | Body uses 2,400 → you eat 2,400 |
| **Calorie Surplus** | Eating slightly *more* than you burn — supports muscle growth & weight gain | Body uses 2,400 → you eat 2,600 |
| **Calorie Deficit** | Eating *less* than you burn — usually causes weight loss over time | Body uses 2,400 → you eat 2,000 |

> 💡 **Note:** If your goal is to build muscle, a small surplus generally works better than a deficit. (If your goal is fat loss instead, a deficit would be more appropriate — adjust based on *your* specific goal.)

---

## 🧱 Macronutrients ("Macros")

The three big categories of food your body needs in **large quantities**.

### 🥩 Protein — "The Bricks"
Protein is the main building and repair material for muscle. Training breaks muscle down; protein provides the material to rebuild it stronger.

**Good Indian protein sources:**
- Eggs, Milk, Curd, Paneer
- Chicken, Fish
- Soy chunks, Tofu
- Dal, Rajma, Chana, Sprouts
- Greek yogurt / high-protein curd

> ⚠️ Dal + rice together give a useful mix of protein and carbs, but most people still need extra protein sources to hit a muscle-building target.

### 🍚 Carbohydrates — "The Construction Workers"
Your body's primary, clean energy source — fuels workouts and daily brain function.

**Good sources:**
- Rice, Roti, Oats
- Potatoes, Sweet potatoes
- Poha, Upma, Idli, Dosa
- Fruits
- Dal and beans

> ✅ Carbs are **not** automatically fattening. Eating more total calories than you need, over time, is what generally causes weight gain.

### 🧈 Fats — "The Operating System"
A slower-burning fuel. Essential for hormone production (especially testosterone, which drives muscle growth), joint health, and absorbing certain vitamins.

**Good sources:**
- Peanuts, Almonds, Cashews, Seeds
- Ghee, Groundnut oil, Mustard oil, Olive oil
- Eggs, Fish, Milk, Curd, Paneer

> ✅ You do **not** need to eliminate fat to get abs.

---

## 🧹 Fiber — "The Cleanup Crew"

The part of carb-containing food that isn't digested. Helps digestion, bowel regularity, and keeps you feeling full.

**Good sources:**
- Vegetables, Fruits
- Oats, Whole-wheat roti
- Dal, Rajma, Chana, Beans, Seeds

🎯 **Target:** roughly **25–35 g per day**

---

## 🔩 Micronutrients (Vitamins & Minerals — "The Spark Plugs")

Small helper nutrients needed in tiny amounts to run internal biological processes. Mostly covered by a varied diet of fruits, vegetables, and dairy.

| Nutrient | Good Sources |
|---|---|
| **Calcium** | Milk, curd, paneer, ragi |
| **Iron** | Beans, leafy vegetables, meat, eggs |
| **Vitamin C** | Amla, guava, oranges, lemon |
| **Vitamin A** | Carrots, spinach, colorful vegetables |
| **Vitamin B12** | Eggs, milk, dairy, fish, meat |
| **Vitamin D** | Sunlight exposure; supplementation if needed (under medical guidance) |
| **Potassium** | Bananas, potatoes, dal, fruits |

> 🥗 A **varied diet** is more useful than chasing one "superfood."

### 🔑 Key Takeaways
- Calories in vs. calories out determines weight gain/loss
- Protein rebuilds muscle, carbs fuel it, fats support hormones
- Fiber, vitamins, and minerals keep everything running smoothly
- No single food is magic — variety and consistency matter most

## Estimated Calories and Protein Requirements

These are approximate, not exact science — think of them as a smart starting point that we'll adjust based on how your body responds.

| Metric | Estimate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Maintenance calories** | ~2,350–2,400 kcal/day | Based on your age, height, weight, and a lightly active lifestyle plus 4–5 workouts/week |
| **Target calories (to build muscle)** | ~2,600–2,700 kcal/day | A modest surplus (~300 kcal above maintenance) — enough to grow muscle without gaining much fat |
| **Protein target** | ~130 g/day (about 2 g per kg body weight) | This is the beginner "muscle-building" range recommended for someone your size |
| **Fat target** | ~70–75 g/day | Roughly 25% of your calories — needed for hormones |
| **Carb target** | ~370–380 g/day | The rest of your calories — fuels your workouts and recovery |
| **Fiber target** | 30–35 g/day | For digestion and overall health |

### How to adjust over time:

- Weigh yourself weekly (same day, same time, ideally morning, empty stomach)
- Gaining less than ~0.25 kg/week → add ~150–200 more calories/day (e.g., extra roti, more rice, a handful of nuts)
- Gaining more than ~0.5 kg/week → you're likely gaining too much fat; reduce by ~150–200 calories/day
- Not gaining at all after 3 weeks → increase food intake

> 📌 **Note:** Muscle grows slowly — expect roughly 0.25–0.5 kg of bodyweight gain per week as a healthy beginner pace.

These numbers are targets, not strict rules. For example, if you eat 125 g protein instead of 130 g, that is completely fine.

**Why are carbs relatively high?** You are trying to build muscle, you are relatively light, and you will be training several days per week. Carbs help you train harder and recover better.

# Weekly Workout Plan

**Split:** Upper/Lower, 4 days/week — ideal for a beginner training at home with limited equipment. Each muscle group gets trained twice a week with enough rest between sessions.

> 💡 **How heavy should you go?** Choose a weight that lets you complete every rep with good form, while feeling you could still do about 2–3 more reps. This is called "leaving reps in reserve" — in simple terms, don't train to failure on every set.

> ⚠️ **Week 1 note:** Some days below carry 6–7 exercises. If that feels like too much in your first week or two, do just 2 sets per exercise instead of the full amount, and build up from there.

---

## 📅 Weekly Schedule

| Day | Workout | Main Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Upper Body A | Chest, back, shoulders, arms |
| Tuesday | Lower Body A + Core A | Legs, glutes, core |
| Wednesday | Rest / light walking | Recovery |
| Thursday | Upper Body B | Chest, back, shoulders, arms |
| Friday | Lower Body B + Core B | Legs, glutes, core |
| Saturday | Optional light cardio + mobility | Fitness and recovery |
| Sunday | Complete rest | Recovery |

This gives most muscle groups approximately **48–72 hours** of recovery before training them hard again.

## 🔥 Warm-Up Routine (before every workout, ~7–8 minutes)

| # | Exercise | Duration / Reps | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jumping jacks or spot jogging | 2 min | Raise body temperature and heart rate |
| 2 | Arm circles + arm swings | 30–45 sec | Gently warm up shoulders and upper body |
| 3 | Shoulder dislocates | 30 sec forward + 30 sec backward | Improve shoulder mobility |
| 4 | Cat-Cow stretch | 8–10 slow cycles | Mobilize the spine and upper back |
| 5 | Band/Towel pull-aparts | 15 reps | Activate upper back and rear shoulders |
| 6 | Hip circles | 8–10 each direction | Mobilize the hips |
| 7 | Leg swings | 10 forward/back + 10 side-to-side each leg | Prepare hips and legs for movement |
| 8 | Bodyweight squats | 10–15 reps | Warm up hips, knees, and legs |
| 9 | Warm-up set | 1 light set | Rehearse your first loaded exercise before working weight |

---

## 🧊 Cool-Down / Stretching (once, after the full workout, ~5–8 minutes)

| # | Exercise | Duration / Reps | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Child's pose | 30–60 sec | Relax the body; gently stretch back, hips, shoulders |
| 2 | Doorway chest stretch | 30 sec each side | Stretch chest and front of shoulders |
| 3 | Shoulder cross-body stretch | 30 sec each side | Stretch rear shoulder and upper back |
| 4 | Quad stretch | 30 sec each side | Stretch front of thighs |

| 6 | Hamstring stretch | 30 sec | Stretch back of thighs |
| 7 | Deep breathing | 5 slow breaths | Gradually relax; bring breathing/heart rate down |

## 💪 MONDAY: Upper Body A

**1. Dumbbell Floor Press** *(Chest, Triceps, Shoulders)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Lie flat on your back, knees bent, feet flat on the floor
    - Hold a dumbbell in each hand at chest level, elbows bent and resting on the floor
    - **Inhale** at the bottom, then **exhale** as you press both dumbbells straight up (not locked out hard)
    - **Inhale** again as you lower slowly until your elbows touch the floor, then repeat
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 8–10
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell (light — focus on control)

**2. Chin-Ups** *(Back, Biceps)*
- **Equipment:** Pull-up bar
- **How to do it:**
    - Grip the bar underhand (palms facing you), hands shoulder-width apart
    - Hang fully with arms straight, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you pull your chest up toward the bar, squeezing back and biceps
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly and fully back to a dead hang
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × max reps (aim for 5–8)
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

**3. Standing Dumbbell Shoulder Press** *(Shoulders, Triceps)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall, feet shoulder-width apart, core tight
    - Hold dumbbells at shoulder height, elbows tucked at a 45° angle, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you press straight overhead until arms lock out
    - **Inhale** as you lower the weights back down under control
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 8–10
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell

**4. Dumbbell Bent-Over Row** *(Upper Back, Lats)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Hold a dumbbell in each hand, hinge forward at the hips to ~45°, knees soft, back flat
    - Let the dumbbells hang straight down under your shoulders, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you pull both dumbbells up toward your waist, squeezing shoulder blades together
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly back down under control
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell

**5. Dumbbell Lateral Raises** *(Side Shoulders / Deltoids)*
- **Equipment:** 2 light dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall, dumbbells at your sides, slight bend in elbows, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height (like a "T")
    - Pause briefly at the top
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly back down — avoid swinging or using momentum
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 12–15
- **Starting weight:** Light dumbbells (approx. 3–4 kg each)

**6. Straight Barbell Bicep Curl** *(Biceps)*
- **Equipment:** Straight barbell + weight plates
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall holding the bar with an underhand grip, elbows pinned to your sides, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you curl the bar up toward your chest, squeezing your biceps
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly back down until arms are straight
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side of the bar total

**7. Close-Grip Push-Ups** *(Triceps, Chest)*
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight
- **How to do it:**
    - Get into a plank position, hands slightly narrower than shoulder-width
    - Keep core braced, body in a straight line, **inhale** as you lower your chest toward the floor, elbows tucked close to your sides
    - **Exhale** as you press back up forcefully to the start
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × max reps (aim 8–12)
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

## 🦵 TUESDAY: Lower Body + Abs

**1. Goblet Squat** *(Quads, Glutes, Core)*
- **Equipment:** 1 dumbbell
- **How to do it:**
    - Hold one dumbbell vertically against your chest with both hands, elbows pointing down
    - Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out
    - **Inhale** as you squat down, pushing hips back and bending knees, going as low as comfortable
    - **Exhale** as you push through your heels to stand back up
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** One dumbbell with a 3kg plate on each side

**2. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** *(Hamstrings, Glutes, Lower Back)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs
    - **Inhale** as you hinge forward at the hips (slight knee bend, back flat), letting the dumbbells slide down your legs to about mid-shin
    - **Exhale** as you drive your hips forward to stand back up
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell

**3. Dumbbell Walking Lunges** *(Quads, Glutes, Balance)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells (or bodyweight)
- **How to do it:**
    - Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides
    - **Inhale** as you step forward into a lunge, lowering your back knee toward the floor without touching it
    - **Exhale** as you push off your front foot into the next lunge on the other leg
    - Continue "walking" forward for the set
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 12 steps per leg
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell (or bodyweight only for week 1)

**4. Standing Dumbbell Calf Raises** *(Calves)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you rise up onto your toes as high as possible, squeezing your calves
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly back down with control
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 15–20
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight or light dumbbells

**5. Hanging Knee Raises** *(Lower Abs, Grip)*
- **Equipment:** Pull-up bar
- **How to do it:**
    - Hang from the bar with arms straight, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you curl your knees up toward your chest, without swinging, rounding your pelvis slightly at the top
    - **Inhale** as you lower your legs slowly under control
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 12–15
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

**6. Reverse Crunch** *(Lower Abs)*
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight / floor mat
- **How to do it:**
    - Lie flat on your back, arms resting at your sides, knees bent at 90°, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you bring your knees toward your chest, lifting your hips slightly off the floor
    - **Inhale** as you lower back down slowly, without letting feet touch the ground between reps
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 15
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

**7. Plank** *(Full Core, Shoulders)*
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight / floor mat
- **How to do it:**
    - Get into a forearm plank, elbows under shoulders, body in a straight line
    - Keep hips level — don't let them sag or pike up
    - Hold the position while breathing normally and steadily throughout — don't hold your breath
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 30–45 sec
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

## 💪 THURSDAY: Upper Body B

**1. Chin-Ups** *(Back, Biceps)*
- **Equipment:** Pull-up bar
- **How to do it:**
    - Grip the bar underhand (palms facing you), **inhale** at the dead hang
    - **Exhale** as you pull your chest up to the bar by driving your elbows down
    - **Inhale** as you lower yourself back down under control
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × max reps (aim 5–8, or negatives)
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

**2. Dumbbell Floor Press** *(Chest, Front Shoulders, Triceps)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Lie flat on your back, knees bent, feet flat
    - Hold a dumbbell in each hand at chest level, elbows bent and resting on the floor, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you press both dumbbells straight up (not locked out hard)
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly until your elbows touch the floor again
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 8–10
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell (light — focus on control)

**3. Bent-Over Barbell Row** *(Back, Biceps, Rear Shoulders)*
- **Equipment:** Straight barbell + plates
- **How to do it:**
    - Hinge forward at the hips to ~45°, knees slightly bent, back flat
    - Hold the bar overhand, hands just outside shoulder-width, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you pull the bar up toward your lower belly, squeezing your shoulder blades
    - **Inhale** as you lower slowly back down under control
- **Sets/Reps:** 4 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side of the bar

**4. Push-Ups** *(Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)*
- **Equipment:** Bodyweight / floor (dumbbell handles optional for extra range)
- **How to do it:**
    - Hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, body in a straight line from head to heels
    - **Inhale** as you lower your chest until it hovers an inch above the floor
    - **Exhale** as you push back up until arms are extended
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × max reps (aim 10–15)
- **Starting weight:** Bodyweight only

**5. Dumbbell Lateral Raises** *(Side Shoulders)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall, dumbbells at your sides, slight bend in elbows, **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you raise both arms out to the sides to shoulder height (like a "T")
    - Pause briefly, then **inhale** as you lower slowly back down — avoid swinging or momentum
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 12–15
- **Starting weight:** Light dumbbells (approx. 3–4 kg each)

**6. Standing Dumbbell Hammer Curls** *(Biceps, Forearms)*
- **Equipment:** 2 dumbbells
- **How to do it:**
    - Stand tall, dumbbells at your sides, palms facing your body (like holding a hammer), **inhale** here
    - **Exhale** as you curl the dumbbells upward without twisting your wrists
    - **Inhale** as you lower them slowly back to the start
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 × 10–12
- **Starting weight:** 3kg plate on each side per dumbbell
