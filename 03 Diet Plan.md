# Diet Plan

This plan relies on affordable, everyday Indian groceries. Meal timing can be shifted by an hour or two to fit your schedule.

---

## 🍳 Meal 1: Breakfast (~8:00 AM)
**Calories: ~580 kcal | Protein: ~32g**

- 60g rolled oats cooked in 250ml toned milk (or warm water)
- 1 sliced medium banana
- 1 tablespoon (15–20g) peanut butter
- **Protein Element:**
  - *Non-Veg:* 3 whole boiled or scrambled eggs
  - *Veg:* 200g Greek yogurt/curd (hung curd), or 1 scoop unflavored whey protein stirred into the oats

---

## 🍎 Meal 2: Mid-Morning Snack (~11:30 AM)
**Calories: ~200 kcal | Protein: ~5g**

- 1 seasonal fruit (apple, orange, or guava)
- 15–20g raw almonds or walnuts
- 1 glass buttermilk (chaas) with roasted jeera

---

## 🍛 Meal 3: Lunch (~1:30 PM)
**Calories: ~650 kcal | Protein: ~40g**

- 2 medium phulkas/rotis (light ghee, ~0.5 tsp total) *or* 1.5 cups cooked brown/white rice (~200g)
- 1 large bowl thick dal (toor, moong, or chana) or rajma/chole (~150g cooked)
- 1 large bowl green sabzi (bhindi, beans, palak, or tindora)
- 1 bowl raw cucumber and tomato salad
- **Primary Protein Element:**
  - *Non-Veg:* 120g chicken breast curry (made with light oil)
  - *Veg:* 100g low-fat paneer bhurji/cubes, or 45g dry soya chunks cooked in curry

---

## 🥪 Meal 4: Evening Pre-Workout Snack (~5:00 PM)
**Calories: ~250 kcal | Protein: ~8g**

- 2 slices whole wheat brown bread with 1 tablespoon peanut butter, *or* 1 cup boiled kala chana chaat (with onion, tomato, lemon)
- 1 cup black coffee or milk chai (with minimal sugar)

---

   *(Workout: 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM)*

---

## 🍲 Meal 5: Dinner — Post-Workout Recovery (~8:30 PM)
**Calories: ~620 kcal | Protein: ~40g**

- 2 medium rotis or 1.5 cups cooked rice
- 1 bowl mixed vegetable sabzi or light dal
- **Primary Protein Element:**
  - *Non-Veg:* 120g fish fillet/chicken breast, or 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites
  - *Veg:* 100g paneer cooked with vegetables, or 40g dry soya chunks pulav
- 1 small bowl curd (dahi, ~100g)

---

## 🥛 Meal 6: Optional Bedtime Snack (~10:30 PM)
**Calories: ~130 kcal | Protein: ~7g**

- 1 glass (200ml) warm toned milk with a pinch of haldi (turmeric)

---

### 📊 Daily Totals
**~2,430 – 2,500 kcal | ~132 – 140g Protein | ~315g Carbs | ~68g Fat**

# Vegetarian & Non-Vegetarian Food Alternatives

| Nutrient Group | Non-Vegetarian Options | Vegetarian / Vegan Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Complete Proteins** (~25–30g protein yield) | • 120g Chicken breast<br>• 140g Fish (Rohu, Katla, Basa)<br>• 4 Whole Eggs + 2 Whites | • 150g Paneer<br>• 45g dry Soya Chunks (boiled)<br>• 200g Low-fat Tofu<br>• 1 scoop Whey Protein |
| **Carbohydrate Swaps** (~30–40g carbs) | • 2 Rotis (Whole wheat)<br>• 1 bowl (150g) Cooked Rice<br>• 150g Boiled Potatoes<br>• 50g Raw Oats / Poha | Same as Non-Veg (Rotis, Rice, Sweet Potatoes, Millets, Oats, Bananas) |
| **Healthy Fats** (~10–14g fats) | • 1 tbsp Ghee / Mustard Oil<br>• 2 Whole Eggs<br>• 20g Almonds/Walnuts | • 1 tbsp Ghee / Coconut Oil<br>• 1 tbsp (20g) Peanut Butter<br>• 20g Almonds, Walnuts, or Roasted Peanuts |

> **Note on Soya Chunks:** Soya chunks contain over 50% pure protein by weight, make an inexpensive vegetarian staple, and are safe to consume at 40–50 grams per day.

### 💰 Affordable Foods to Prioritize

You do not need expensive "fitness foods." Good budget-friendly options:

- Eggs, Milk, Curd
- Soy chunks, Dal, Chana, Rajma
- Peanuts
- Rice, Roti, Oats
- Bananas
- Seasonal vegetables, Potatoes
- Homemade paneer when practical

> 💊 **On supplements:** You don't need them — but a basic whey protein scoop (mixed in milk) can help conveniently hit your 130g/day protein target on busy days. It's optional, not required.
